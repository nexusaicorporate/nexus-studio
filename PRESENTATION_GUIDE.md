## Presentation Structure (30-45 minutes)

**Why Now**: Generative AI adoption is mainstream. Model pricing is commoditizing. Multi-provider is inevitable.

### 2. Problem Statement (4 min)
**Show**:
- Vendor lock-in pain (MidJourney → Runway switching costs)

- Skill ceiling (learning each provider's API)

**Market Data**:

## Handling Q&A

### Q: "Isn't this just a UI wrapper?"
**A**: "No. We've built deep integrations with 8+ providers. We handle authentication, rate limiting, cost calculation, and intelligent routing. When one provider's API breaks, our users don't notice—we fail over to alternatives. That's infrastructure, not a wrapper."

### Q: "Why can't MidJourney/Runway build this?"
**A**: "They're single-provider by definition. If MidJourney added Runway's features, they cannibalize image revenue. Our business model *depends* on multi-provider parity. That's a structural advantage."

### Q: "How do you make money if you're cheaper than competitors?"
**A**: "We make money by reducing switching costs. Instead of users paying $180 across 5 tools, they pay $99 to Nexus. We win via convenience, not vendor lock-in. Plus, we own the relationship—that's where enterprise revenue grows."

### Q: "What about data security/compliance?"
**A**: "Enterprise users need it. That's why we're building SSO, audit logs, and data residency as add-on tiers. Healthcare/finance customers will pay $5K+/month for that. It's a future revenue stream."

### Q: "What if a provider cuts you off?"
**A**: "We immediately activate our fallback chain—swap to another provider, notify users, update docs. We've already modeled this. The abstraction layer is the point."

### Q: "How much traction do you have?"
**A**: (Vary by actual status)
- Early: "We've validated product-market fit with 500 beta users. NPS is 45, retention is 70%."
- Later: "We have 10K free users, 50 Pro customers, and 3 enterprise pilots in negotiations."

### Q: "Why should I believe the financial projections?"
**A**: "These are conservative. We're assuming 2-3% free-to-Pro conversion (SaaS average is 5%+), 12% Studio upgrade rates (industry: 15%+), and healthy churn. We've modeled downside cases too."
