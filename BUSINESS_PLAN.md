# Nexus AI Studio — Comprehensive Business Plan

## 1. Company Overview

### Mission
Nexus AI Studio empowers creators, marketers, and enterprises to harness the full potential of generative AI without vendor lock-in, providing a unified platform where the best models compete on merit, not ecosystem lock.

### Vision (3-Year)
Become the **default operating system** for AI-powered content creation—trusted by 100K+ creators and enterprises to manage $500M+ in annual AI spending.

### Core Values
- **Creator-First**: Build for those who create, not corporations
- **Transparency**: Show costs, model performance, and choices clearly
- **Openness**: Multi-provider architecture, no proprietary lock-in
- **Privacy**: User data is sacred, never trained on without consent
- **Excellence**: Maintain highest standards for UX, performance, security

## 7. Competitive Analysis

### Market Dynamics

**Incumbent Players** (strong in single domain)
- MidJourney: Best-in-class image quality, but image-only, expensive, closed
- Runway: Industry-leading video, but single provider, high cost, no desktop
- Leonardo AI: Cost-effective, but limited video, no enterprise features

**API-First Players** (fragmented developer experience)
- Replicate: Great for developers, but high latency, no UI, learning curve
- Modal: GPU infrastructure provider, not end-user platform

**Advantage: Nexus**
1. **Multi-provider routing** = lower cost, better availability
2. **Unified UX** = no tool switching, faster creative iteration
3. **Desktop + Web** = work anywhere, flexible deployment
4. **Enterprise-ready** = audit logs, SSO, SLA, compliance
5. **Open ecosystem** = API-first, Zapier, integrations

## 8. Risk Management

### Market Risks
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
