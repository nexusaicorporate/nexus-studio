# Nexus AI Studio — Investor Pitch Deck

## Executive Summary

**Nexus AI Studio** is a unified generative AI platform enabling creators, marketers, and enterprises to produce high-quality visual and video content at scale. We aggregate multiple AI providers (OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic, Modal) through one intuitive interface, eliminating vendor lock-in while providing superior model selection, cost optimization, and creative control.

### Investment Highlights
- **Market Size**: $13.1B addressable AI creativity market, 41% YoY growth
- **Target Users**: 2M+ creators, 500K+ marketing teams, 50K+ enterprises
- **Revenue Model**: Freemium SaaS + Pro/Studio tiers + Enterprise contracts
- **Pricing Tiers**: Free (limited), Pro ($29/mo), Studio ($99/mo), Enterprise (custom)
- **Financial Projection**: $1M ARR by Month 18, 40-65% gross margins, $50K MRR by Month 14

## Problem

### Market Gaps
1. **Vendor Lock-in**: Users trapped in single-provider ecosystems (MidJourney, Runway)
2. **Cost Blindness**: No visibility into per-request costs or provider pricing
3. **Fragmentation**: Need 5+ separate tools for complete creative workflow
4. **Skill Ceiling**: High learning curve for each provider's API and syntax
5. **Enterprise Gaps**: No audit logs, SSO, SLAs, or compliance tools

### Market Validation
- Creators spend average $300+/month across 3-5 tools
- 78% of users report dissatisfaction with vendor lock-in (industry survey)
- $2.4B annual spend on "retooling" when switching providers
