# Nexus AI Studio — Product Rollout & Launch Plan

## Executive Overview

This document outlines the phased rollout strategy for Nexus AI Studio from **Month 1 (Seed Close) through Month 18 (Series A Readiness)**. The plan prioritizes product-market fit validation, creator acquisition, and enterprise pipeline building.

## Risk & Contingency Planning

### Key Risks in Rollout

| Risk | Phase | Mitigation |
|------|-------|-----------|
| Slower free → Pro conversion (below 3%) | 1-2 | Improve onboarding UX, implement feature gates |
| Inability to hire quality engineers | 2-3 | Start recruiting early, offer above-market equity |
| Competition from new entrants | 1-3 | Focus on product differentiation, move fast |
| Enterprise sales cycles extend | 3 | Keep SMB/Pro growth as fallback revenue |
| Product stability issues at scale | 2 | Invest in infrastructure + observability early |

### Contingency Options

**If Free → Pro Conversion Underperforms (< 2%)**
- Increase focus on inbound marketing (SEO, content)
- Adjust Pro pricing down ($19-24/month)
- Implement free-to-paid workflow experiments

**If Enterprise Sales Stall**
- Accelerate SMB/Agency focus
- Build case studies from Pro users
- Expand pilot program to 5-10 companies

**If Fundraising Delayed**
- Cut discretionary expenses (marketing, travel)
- Extend timeline by 3-6 months
- Pursue smaller round ($250K bridge) or revenue-based financing
